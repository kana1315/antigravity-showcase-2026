📊 Key Empirical Findings

  • The Location vs Lease Decay Paradox: Buyers in Mature towns pay a 29.6% price premium per square meter (S$5,256 vs S$4,055) despite holding an asset with 9.0 fewer years of remaining lease on average (68.9 years vs 77.9 years).
  • Statistically Rigorous Valuation Gradient: Controlled OLS regression reveals that each additional year of remaining lease contributes +S$40.97 / sqm, whereas mature estate location imparts a massive +S$1,590.20 / sqm standalone premium.
  • The Vertical Storey Gradient: Units on floors 49–51 trade at an average of S$10,345 / sqm, representing a 145.2% premium over ground-to-3rd floor units (S$4,218 / sqm), driven by iconic projects like Pinnacle@Duxton.
  • Million-Dollar Specialization: Exactly 9 transactions in the sample exceeded S$1,000,000. All were located in central/mature hubs (Central Area, Bishan, Clementi, Kallang) and 88.9% had >92 years remaining lease on upper-tier floors.

💡 Strategic Recommendations

  • For First-Time Buyers (Ages 25–35): Prioritize Non-Mature estates (e.g., Punggol, Sengkang, Woodlands) with >80 years remaining lease. You avoid the S$150k mature location tax while securing superior lease preservation for long-term equity growth.
  • For Upgraders & Aging Asset Owners: Liquidate mature flats when remaining lease reaches the 65-year threshold. Below 60 years, commercial banks restrict loan-to-value (LTV) and CPF withdrawal rules tighten, accelerating valuation erosion.
  • For Family-Size Space Maximizers: In Non-Mature towns, 5-Room and Executive units exhibit superior economies of scale (averaging S$3,926–S$4,113/sqm vs S$5,274/sqm for 2-Room), offering the highest square footage per dollar.
  • For Housing Policy Analysts: Expand Voluntary Early Round Redevelopment Scheme (VERS) transparency to prevent speculative mispricing in aging mature estates approaching the 50-year lease mark.
